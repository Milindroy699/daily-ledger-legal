Daily Ledger — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Daily Ledger is a habit and daily-planning app. It works fully offline; cloud features are optional.

What we store

  • Your ledger (rituals, goals, daily ticks, water, notes, journal, settings). By default it stays on your device, encrypted at rest.
  • If you create a cloud account: your email address (or Google / Apple sign-in identifier) and your ledger, stored with our backend provider (Supabase) and protected by per-user access rules. It is not end-to-end encrypted.
  • If you use Circle (sharing with friends): the display name and progress you choose to share with people you connect with.

Optional features

  • Google Drive backup uses only the app-private drive.appdata folder. We cannot see your other Drive files.
  • Notifications are local reminders scheduled on your device.
  • Face ID / biometrics are handled by your device; we never receive biometric data.
  • Exports (JSON/CSV/PDF) are created on request and saved or shared where you choose.

What we do not do

No ads, no tracking, no analytics SDKs, and no sale or sharing of your data with third parties.

Deleting your data

In the app: More → "Delete account & data" permanently removes your account and cloud ledger. See how to delete your account. Device-only data is removed by deleting the app.

Children

Daily Ledger is not directed to children under 13.
